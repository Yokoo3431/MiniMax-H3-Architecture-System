## 2. 4-Dimension Evaluation Matrix

### 1. Geometry Integrity (建筑体量与结构完整性)
- **Check**: Detects whether columns, balconies, rooflines, or floorplates warp or melt across video frames.
- **Metric**: Structural SSIM stability score across keyframes.

### 2. Material Consistency (材质漂移审计)
- **Check**: Ensures glass curtainwalls do not morph into concrete or timber during camera motion.
- **Metric**: Optical flow color histogram drift index.

### 3. Camera Perspective (建筑摄影虚线与透视逻辑)
- **Check**: Verifies vertical architectural lines remain straight (two-point perspective principle).
- **Metric**: Hough transform vertical line angle variance.

### 4. Lighting & Contrast (建筑可视化灯光与大气氛围)
- **Check**: Verifies twilight sky contrast and interior warm light illumination.
- **Metric**: Luminance distribution & color temperature balance score.
