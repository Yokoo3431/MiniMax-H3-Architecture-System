## 3. Dynamic Optimization Algorithms

1. **VRAM Optimizer**: Prioritizes keeping the 19.53GB INT8 ConvRot DiT model in GPU VRAM while offloading the Qwen3-VL text encoder to host RAM post-conditioning.
2. **Resolution Planner**: Auto-scales non-standard architectural rendering ratios (e.g. 21:9 ultra-wide or 4:3 portrait) to nearest VRAM-safe multiples of 64.
3. **Timestep Optimizer**: Uses sigmoidal noise schedule density to reduce steps from 50 $\rightarrow$ 25 without loss of architectural facade details.
