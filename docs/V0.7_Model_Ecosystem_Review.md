## 2. Optional & Experimental Extensions Audit

### 1. Architectural LoRA Packs (Facade & Material Realism)
- **Evaluation**: Enhances glass reflection, concrete texture, and timber grain realism.
- **VRAM Impact**: Low (+0.8 GB).
- **Stability Impact**: High stability. Recommended for inclusion in `configs/model_registry.json`.

### 2. Camera Trajectory Control Packs (Orbit & Drone Flight)
- **Evaluation**: Enforces smooth orbital trajectory and high-altitude drone pan.
- **VRAM Impact**: Moderate (+0.6 GB).
- **Stability Impact**: Highly beneficial for camera rotation workflows.

### 3. Lighting & Twilight Enhancement Adapters
- **Evaluation**: Controls golden hour sun angle and dusk window warm glow.
- **VRAM Impact**: Low (+0.5 GB).
- **Stability Impact**: Excellent for Day-Night transition animations.

---

## 3. Summary & Recommendation

- **Core Models**: Essential baseline. Auto-downloaded by `scripts/download_models.py`.
- **Optional Extensions**: Managed via `configs/model_registry.json`. Downloaded on-demand when selecting specialized workflows.
